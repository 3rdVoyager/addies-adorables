          ## About

          Addie started crocheting as a way to unwind after a long day. What began as a quiet hobby quickly turned into something more when friends started asking for their own little handmade friends. Addie's Adorables was born from a love of handmade gifts and the belief that a single, small crocheted friend can bring a lot of joy.

          Every order is made by hand, one at a time, so no two adorables are ever exactly alike.
